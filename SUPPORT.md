## Ask for help

Use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/YazeKT/PinHarbor/issues) for reproducible
bugs and focused feature requests. Search existing issues first and use the
appropriate issue form.

When reporting a bug, include your Windows version, Go version, PinHarbor commit,
launcher output, expected behavior, and the smallest safe reproduction. Remove
business data, search terms, email addresses, proxy URLs, credentials, and local
paths before posting.

## Security and legal questions

Do not use a public issue for a vulnerability; follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).
Questions about whether a particular collection or outreach campaign is lawful
require advice appropriate to your jurisdiction. The project cannot provide
individual legal clearance.

## Scope

PinHarbor is maintained as a Windows-local, single-operator application. Public
hosting, multi-user authentication, managed scraping, and guaranteed support
for upstream SaaS infrastructure are outside the supported product scope.
