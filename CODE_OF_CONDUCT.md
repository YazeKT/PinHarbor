# Community code of conduct

## Our standard

PinHarbor's community should be practical, respectful, and safe for people of
all backgrounds and experience levels. Constructive disagreement is welcome;
harassment, threats, discrimination, sexualized conduct, doxxing, spam, and
deliberate disruption are not.

Examples of welcome behavior include:

- giving specific, actionable feedback;
- respecting different constraints and levels of experience;
- acknowledging upstream and community contributions;
- protecting private information found in logs or scrape results;
- accepting a maintainer's decision about project scope gracefully.

## Scope

This standard applies in repository issues, pull requests, discussions, and any
other space officially representing PinHarbor.

## Enforcement

Report conduct concerns privately through the contact options on the
[maintainer's GitHub profile](https://github.com/YazeKT). Do not include private
business data or unrelated personal information. The maintainer may edit or
remove content, close interactions, or temporarily or permanently restrict
participation when necessary to protect the project and its community.

Reports will be reviewed in good faith and handled as privately as practical.
