# Legal and responsible use

PinHarbor is a general-purpose local collection tool. This document explains
important operator responsibilities; it is not legal advice.

## Your responsibility

You are responsible for deciding whether a collection is lawful and permitted
where you operate and where the people or businesses in the data are located.
Before running a collection, review applicable law, contractual restrictions,
website terms, and any rules imposed by your network or proxy provider.

Automated access to a public page does not mean every use of the page or its
data is permitted. PinHarbor does not bypass account access controls, and you
must not use it to evade technical restrictions, overwhelm services, obtain
non-public data, or facilitate harassment, discrimination, fraud, spam, or
other unlawful activity.

## Collected data

Business listings can contain personal or regulated information, particularly
names, direct telephone numbers, email addresses, reviews, and sole-trader
addresses. Depending on the context and jurisdiction, you may need a lawful
basis, notices, consent, suppression lists, retention limits, access controls,
or a process for correction and deletion requests.

If you use results for email, telephone, SMS, or other outreach, you are
responsible for the rules that govern that channel and recipient. Do not assume
that a publicly listed contact detail is consent to receive marketing.

## Accuracy and suitability

Web pages change, listings may be incomplete, and automated extraction can be
wrong. Email discovery is best-effort and can associate an address with the
wrong entity. Verify important data before relying on it, contacting someone,
or making a decision that affects a person or business.

## Local security

PinHarbor's supported configuration is a single-operator local application.
It has no built-in user authentication. Do not expose port `8080` to the public
internet. Protect `webdata/`, proxy credentials, exported CSV files, backups,
and any system on which PinHarbor runs.
